# Crops

The crops Feudum adds or changes, and the mechanic that describes each. Every crop grows within a range of air temperatures, which sets the seasons and climates it grows in, as the [seasons](../mechanics/seasons.md#additions-and-changes) and [temperature](../mechanics/temperature.md#world-effects) documents describe. Wild plants and trees are in the [plants index](../plants/README.md).

## New crops

| Crop      | Grown for                                                                                    | Mechanic                                                         |
|:----------|:---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|:-----------------------------------------------------------------|
| Rye       | Grain for bread and ale. Rye harvested in the rain can carry ergot, which the mill sifts out | [Disease](../mechanics/disease.md#medicinal-plants-and-rye)      |
| Barley    | Malt for small ale, ale, and beer, and straw                                                 | [Thirst](../mechanics/thirst.md#drinks)                          |
| Hops      | Beer, which hops keep for weeks                                                              | [Thirst](../mechanics/thirst.md#drinks)                          |
| Grapevine | Grapes for wine, where the climate lets them grow                                            | [Thirst](../mechanics/thirst.md#additions-and-changes)           |
| Cabbage   | Sauerkraut                                                                                   | [Food spoilage](../mechanics/food-spoilage.md#preservation)      |
| Beans     | Dried beans, which keep through winter                                                       | [Food spoilage](../mechanics/food-spoilage.md#preservation)      |
| Flax      | Fiber for linen                                                                              | [Temperature](../mechanics/temperature.md#additions-and-changes) |
| Hemp      | Fiber for sackcloth, retted and dressed like flax                                            | [Vermin](../mechanics/vermin.md#additions-and-changes)           |
| Woad      | Blue dye, grown in the dyer's dye garden                                                     | [Roles](../roles/README.md)                                      |
| Madder    | Red dye, grown in the dyer's dye garden                                                      | [Roles](../roles/README.md)                                      |
| Weld      | Yellow dye, grown in the dyer's dye garden                                                   | [Roles](../roles/README.md)                                      |

## Changed vanilla crops

| Crop       | Change                                                                                       | Mechanic                                                                                                           |
|:-----------|:---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|:-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Every crop | Grows only within its range of air temperatures, and frost kills it unless it is frost-hardy | [Seasons](../mechanics/seasons.md#additions-and-changes), [Temperature](../mechanics/temperature.md#world-effects) |
| Wheat      | Harvesting it also gives straw                                                               | [Sanitation](../mechanics/sanitation.md#livestock-waste)                                                           |

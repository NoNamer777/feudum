# Mobs

Each document describes one mob: how it lives and behaves, and the items, blocks, and behaviors it depends on. The list covers new mobs and the vanilla mobs Feudum changes. Particle swarms such as flies, mosquitoes, and fleas are not mobs, and neither are entities such as the shrouded body.

## Planned

These mobs have no document yet:

- **Rat**: the black rat, which nests near food stores, raids them at night, and carries the fleas that bring plague.
- **Mouse**: nests in the walls and thatch of a home, and nibbles the food in its larder.
- **Weevil**: a tiny beetle that breeds in stored grain, flour, and dried beans, and eats them.
- **Clothes moth**: a tiny moth whose larvae eat holes in stored wool, silk, and fur, as cloth and as clothing.

### Changed vanilla mobs

- **Cat**: hunts rats and mice around its home, and sometimes leaves a dead rat as its morning gift.
- **Tamed wolf**: attacks rats and mice on sight.
- **Ratting dog**: a tamed wolf that follows the ratoner, hunts rats down, and digs out their nests.
- **Sheep**: grows a fleece once a year, which shearing takes as raw fleece instead of wool blocks. Drops a sheepskin when slaughtered unshorn, and only mutton once shorn.
- **Fox**: drops a fox pelt, and raids rabbit warrens.
- **Horse, donkey, mule, camel, and llama**: wear blankets, and the llama's carpet becomes a wool blanket.

# Roles

Each document describes one villager role: the work it does for the village, and the items, blocks, and behaviors it depends on.

## Planned

These roles have no document yet:

- **Farmer**: plows and sows in spring and harvests in autumn, so the village has food to store for winter. The farmer also grows flax, hemp, cotton, and teasels, and rets the flax and hemp.
- **Woodcutter**: fells trees, mostly in winter, and supplies the firewood that heats the village's homes.
- **Builder**: raises and repairs the village's buildings, mostly in summer, and digs its wells.
- **Herder**: tends the village's livestock, keeps their troughs full and the herds fed and sheltered through winter, and washes and shears the sheep in late spring.
- **Tailor**: sews and mends the village's clothing, gambesons, and horse blankets, warm for winter and light for summer, and waxes linen for waxed coats.
- **Herbalist**: gathers medicinal plants in their season, and makes simple remedies such as teas and poultices for mild illness.
- **Apothecary**: buys herbs and minerals, and brews stronger remedies for serious illness in a shop.
- **Barber-surgeon**: treats the body rather than the illness: bloodletting, setting bones, pulling teeth, and cutting hair.
- **Brewer**: malts barley and brews small ale, ale, beer, and mead, so the village always has something safe to drink.
- **Orchardist**: tends apple and pear trees, and presses their fruit into cider and perry.
- **Vintner**: tends the vineyard and makes wine, where the climate lets grapes grow.
- **Beekeeper**: keeps bees in straw skeps for honey and wax.
- **Cooper**: makes barrels, casks, buckets, tubs, troughs, fulling troughs, woad vats, and tankards, and turns wooden cups and bowls.
- **Potter**: throws and fires clay jugs, cups, and storage pots.
- **Tanner**: turns hides into leather at the edge of the village, away from homes and water.
- **Leatherworker**: makes waterskins, belts, straps, and saddles from the tanner's leather.
- **Smith**: forges tools, kettles, brewing coppers, barrel hoops, well chains, needles, wool combs, heckles, and copper dye vats.
- **Salter**: boils or sun-dries seawater into salt on the coast.
- **Butcher**: slaughters livestock in the autumn cull, and salts and smokes the meat to keep through winter.
- **Fisher**: catches fish, and salts and dries the catch so it keeps.
- **Dairymaid**: churns milk into butter and presses it into cheese.
- **Miller**: grinds the village's grain into flour at a mill.
- **Baker**: bakes bread every few days, since bread keeps only four.
- **Merchant**: carries the village's surplus to other villages and markets, and brings back what it lacks.
- **Midwife**: delivers the village's children, and nurses the sick who have no household to care for them.
- **Sexton**: digs the graves and keeps the burial ground, so the dead stay buried.
- **Priest**: holds the services and holy days, and consecrates the burial ground.
- **Gong farmer**: empties the village's cesspits at night, and carts the night soil to the laystall.
- **Raker**: clears filth from the streets and refuse from full middens, and carts both to the laystall.
- **Spinner**: combs wool, gins and bows cotton, and spins wool, flax, hemp, and cotton into yarn and thread on a distaff and spindle or a spinning wheel.
- **Weaver**: weaves yarn into wool cloth, linen, canvas, sackcloth, and fustian on a treadle loom.
- **Fuller**: thickens woven wool in urine and fuller's earth, and stretches it to dry on tenter frames.
- **Dyer**: grows woad, madder, and weld in a dye garden, and dyes yarn and cloth in heated vats.
- **Whitster**: bleaches linen with lye and buttermilk, and lays it out in the sun on a bleaching field.
- **Carpenter**: builds looms, spinning wheels, tenter frames, sewing tables, and the wooden tools of the cloth trades, as well as carts, privy seats, and furniture.
- **Ratoner**: hunts the village's rats and mice by night with a ratting dog, destroys their nests, and sets traps and ratsbane.
- **Mason**: cuts and lays stone, and carves the staddles that raise a granary out of reach of rats.
- **Cordwainer**: makes turnshoes and boots from the tanner's leather, and mends them.
- **Skinner**: taws rabbit hides, sheepskins, and fox pelts into fur with alum and salt, and makes fur cloaks, fur linings, and the lining of the pelisse.
- **Glover**: makes mittens, gloves, and fur-lined gloves.
- **Hatter**: makes felt and felt hats, plaits straw hats, and finishes fur hats with the skinner's fur.
- **Warrener**: keeps rabbits in pillow mounds for meat and fur, and traps the foxes that raid them.
- **Heckler**: dresses retted flax and hemp: breaks the stalks, scutches the woody core away, and heckles the fiber into long line for spinning and coarse tow for sackcloth.
- **Shearman**: raises the nap of fulled cloth with teasels, and shears it smooth into finished cloth.
- **Roper**: spins hemp into twine, and lays twine into rope on a ropewalk.
- **Silkwoman**: rears silkworms on mulberry leaves, reels and throws their silk, and weaves silk cloth and ribbons, as the silkwomen of medieval London did.

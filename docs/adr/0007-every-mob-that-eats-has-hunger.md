# Every mob that eats has hunger

Every mob that eats, players included, carries a stomach, an energy store, and a diet, tuned by a data-driven diet profile for its entity type. Players, villagers, and livestock also track food groups whose deficiencies wear a body down. Keeping vanilla's one food bar for players alone would have been simpler. But a village lives on feeding its people and herds through the winter, and a wolf pack only raids the flocks if it has a reason to. One model gives villagers, livestock, and predators that reason in the same way, and food groups give the village economy a varied diet to produce rather than one crop. The cost is that every eating mob carries the simulation while loaded, and every new entity type needs a diet profile or an explicit exemption. Villagers, Village economy, and Village simulation must also define how villagers choose their meals, where the food comes from, and what an unloaded village loses to a poor harvest.

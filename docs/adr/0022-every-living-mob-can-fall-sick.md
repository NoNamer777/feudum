# Every living mob can fall sick

Every living mob whose body profile carries an illness block can fall sick: it catches named diseases that run a course of days, act through its needs and its body, and spread to others. Limiting illness to players and villagers would have been simpler, since only they wash, take remedies, and lie in bed. But the reasons illness matters reach past humans. Foul water and deep dung sicken a herd, murrain sweeps a byre and empties a village's larder, a sick horse passes glanders to its groom, and wild boar bring swine fever to the village pigs. One model makes a herd's health a matter of care, as Health and the needs already do. Hanging illness on the body profile lets each species opt in, tune its resistance, and be named as a host by the diseases that infect it, without tags of its own. The cost is that every living mob carries its diseases and immunities while loaded, and every body profile needs an illness block or an explicit exemption. Garments, bedding, drink containers, troughs, and chunks carry disease state too. Villagers must decide how villagers care for the sick, and Village simulation must run outbreaks while a village is unloaded.

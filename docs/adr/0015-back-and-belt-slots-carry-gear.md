# Back and belt slots carry gear

A body gets two more slots, the back and the belt, for 18 in all: the back holds a slung shield, a slung bow, or a back quiver, and the belt holds a hip quiver or a sheathed weapon. Keeping the 16 slots of [ADR 0013](0013-a-helm-slot-sits-over-the-head-armor.md) would have been simpler, with quivers and spare weapons carried in the inventory like any item. But a shield slung on the back still covered a soldier from behind, an archer drew from the quiver at the hip or the back, and a sword hung at the belt where it could be drawn at once. Slots of their own let [Weapons](../design/content/weapons.md) give a slung shield its cover, draw ammunition from a quiver before the inventory, and render carried gear on the body. The cost is two more saved slots on every player and humanoid mob, two more slots on the paper-doll panel and the armor stand, and a rule for how a body draws from them, which [Inventory](https://github.com/NoNamer777/feudum/issues/83) owns.

# Salt is a need as well as a load

Every species whose diet profile tracks it carries a salt level, which the salt amount of what it eats and drinks fills and time and sweat drain, and a body whose level runs low becomes deficient. People, grazers, and browsers track it by default. Treating salt only as the load of Thirst would have been simpler, since that already exists and vanilla has no salt at all. But a load alone makes salt a harm to avoid, and history ran the other way: villages traded, taxed, and boiled for salt because bodies and herds needed it. One amount that dries a body out when there is too much and leaves it deficient when there is too little makes a salted winter, a seasoned pottage, and a lick in the pasture each matter. It also gives an inland village a reason to trade and a deer a reason to come to a spring. The cost is that every tracking body carries one more level while loaded, and every diet profile must say whether it tracks salt. Foods must give every food a natural salt amount, Wildlife must lead wild herbivores to natural licks, and Village simulation must draw salt from an unloaded village's stock.

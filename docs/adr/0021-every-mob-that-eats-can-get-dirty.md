# Every mob that eats can get dirty

Every mob whose diet profile carries a dirt block gets dirty: grime, mud, and bloodstain build on its body, and a smell derived from them shows to villagers and carries to animals downwind. Giving dirt to players and villagers alone would have been simpler, since only they wear garments, wash in tubs, and answer to standing. But the reasons dirt matters reach past humans. An unmucked barn turns its stock filthy, pigs wallow in mud to cool off, and a sheep's dirt passes to its fleece at shearing. A wolf scents a wounded deer as a deer scents an unwashed hunter. Hanging dirt on the diet profile, as Relief does, lets each species opt in and tune its rates without tags of its own. The cost is that every eating mob carries three more values while loaded, and every diet profile needs a dirt block or an explicit exemption. Wildlife and Stealth must read scent, Villagers must decide when villagers wash, and Village simulation must bring dirt forward while a village is unloaded.

# Lordship attaches to manors, not villages

A lord holds a manor, a set of a village's buildings with the villagers whose homes are in them, rather than the whole village. A village can hold several manors under different lords, and stays one community with one name and one territory. One lord per village would have been simpler, but it would have made sharing a village in multiplayer all or nothing. Medieval villages were often divided between several manors in the same way, which gave lords a reason to compete and villagers more room. Village economy and Realms will build on the manor as the unit of lordship, so changing it later would mean redesigning both. The cost is a second unit to track: buildings and villagers each belong to a manor or to the village holding, and every lordship rule applies per manor.

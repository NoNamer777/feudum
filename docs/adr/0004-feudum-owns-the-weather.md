# Feudum owns the weather

Feudum computes the weather from the world seed, the position, and the Feudum time, and keeps vanilla weather at clear with the weather cycle locked off. Vanilla keeps one rain level and one thunder level per dimension and advances them by ticks. It could give neither per-biome weather nor fronts that travel, and catch-up would have to store or replay its history. Computing the weather lets catch-up, other mechanics, and weather signs read any past or future moment without storing anything. The cost is that Feudum renders its own precipitation and sky, and replaces `/weather` with overrides kept on a timeline so the computed weather stays consistent. Editing a climate profile or a weather setting also rewrites the weather of every date, past dates included.

# Every mob that eats has relief

Every mob that has hunger and thirst also has relief: a bladder and bowels that fill from what the body digests and can't absorb, tuned by a relief block in its diet profile, and waste that lands where the body relieves. Leaving waste out, or giving it to animals alone as a dung drop, would have been simpler, since vanilla has no relief at all. But Fluids already counts waste among the sources that foul water, and Farming needs manure to feed its fields. Only a body that has to go somewhere makes a privy uphill of the well, a chamber pot emptied into the street, or a barn left unmucked a choice with consequences. The cost is that every eating mob carries two more stores and a relieving goal while loaded, and every diet profile needs a relief block or an explicit exemption. Waste layers add fouling sources that Fluids must track, Villagers must define when and where a villager relieves, and Village simulation must fill privy pits and dung heaps while a village is unloaded.

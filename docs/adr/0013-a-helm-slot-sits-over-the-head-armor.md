# A helm slot sits over the head's armor

The head gets a 16th slot, the helm, over its armor layer: the armor slot holds a mail coif, and the helm slot holds an open or great helm. Keeping the 15 slots of [ADR 0012](0012-bodies-wear-clothing-in-three-layers.md) would have been simpler, with a helm fitted at the anvil with a coif or aventail into one piece, the way a lined garment takes its lining. But a knight put on an arming cap, a mail coif, and a helm as three separate things, and a coif alone was common armor for those who had no helm. A slot of its own lets a body wear either or both, and take off the helm without losing the coif. The cost is a 16th saved slot on every player and humanoid mob, a helm slot on the paper-doll panel, and a hooded cloak whose hood must come down under a helm.

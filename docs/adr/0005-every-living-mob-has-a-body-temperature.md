# Every living mob has a body temperature

Every living mob, players included, carries a body temperature and a wetness, tuned by a data-driven thermal profile for its entity type. Limiting the mechanic to players would have been simpler, and villagers and animals could have reacted to the weather through their own rules. But a management game lives on its villagers and livestock surviving the seasons. One model lets a barn, a hearth, or a sheared fleece matter to every creature in the same way, and the cues on a herd tell the player how it fares. The cost is that every mob carries the simulation while loaded, and every new entity type needs a thermal profile or an explicit exemption. Villagers and Village simulation must also define how villagers react and what an unloaded village loses to the cold.

# Bodies wear clothing in three layers

Players and humanoid mobs wear clothing in three layers on each region (an underlayer, a clothing layer, and armor) plus a cloak over everything, for 15 slots across head, chest, legs, feet, and hands. Keeping vanilla's four equipment slots, each holding either a garment or a piece of armor, would have been simpler. But Body temperature makes warmth, wind, and rain matter to every body, and one slot per region forces a choice between a warm tunic and mail that no medieval body had to make. Layers let a linen shirt, a wool tunic, mail, and a cloak each do their part, and give each garment its own wetness, so a good cloak keeps the tunic dry. The cost is a paper-doll inventory with layer tabs in place of vanilla's armor column, 15 slots of saved equipment on every player and humanoid mob, and garment wetness to track on each of them. Armor must define its pieces for the outer layer, and Villagers and Village economy must keep a village clothed.

# Fish are mobs with populations of their own

Every fish in Feudum is a real mob that swims in the water. A small schooling species lives as one shoal mob that carries a count, and fish breed, grow, and die by the habitat of the water body they live in. An abstract stock for each water body would have been cheaper and simpler: a density for each species that catches draw down and that regrows, with visible fish only as scenery. It would have run in unloaded chunks like the river discharge of Fluids and kept the sea free of entities. But a stock is a number the player never meets. Real fish make a salmon run piling up below a weir, a pike lying in the weed, a shoal flickering under the gulls, and a pond gasping before a winter kill things the player can see and act on. The bait, the net, and the trap then catch the fish that are really there. The cost is that every fish carries its profiles and simulation while loaded, so shoals must stand in for schooling fish to keep entity counts low. Every water chunk must catch up its fish when it loads, and migrating fish must travel in transit across unloaded chunks. World generation must stock every water body to its carrying capacity, and Village simulation must fish an unloaded village's waters by the same rules.

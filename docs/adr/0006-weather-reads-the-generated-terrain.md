# Weather reads the generated terrain

Wind speeds up over ridges and windward slopes and drops in valleys and lee slopes, and fronts drop more precipitation on the windward side of high terrain than in its lee. Feudum computes this exposure from the base height of its world generation, which follows from the world seed alone, rather than from the blocks in the world. Reading the blocks would need loaded chunks and stored history, and would break the rule of [ADR 0004](0004-feudum-owns-the-weather.md) that the weather at any place and moment can be computed. Ignoring terrain would keep weather independent of world generation, but would leave mountains with no wind of their own and no rain shadow. The cost is that a hill a player levels keeps its original wind. World generation must also always offer a base height computable from the seed alone, so it can never shape terrain by generation order or stored state. Placed blocks act on the wind only through wind shelter, at the entity or block that feels it.

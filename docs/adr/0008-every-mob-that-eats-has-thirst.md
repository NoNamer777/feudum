# Every mob that eats has thirst

Every mob that has hunger also has thirst: a hydration store that drinks fill through the shared stomach, and salt and foul loads from what it drinks, tuned by a thirst block in its diet profile. Limiting thirst to players would have been simpler, since vanilla has no thirst at all. But a village has to water its herds as well as feed them. One model makes a well, a trough kept free of ice in winter, or a pond fouled by a pen matter to every creature in the same way. A river becomes a place where predators and prey meet. The cost is that every eating mob carries a second store and a drinking goal while loaded, and every diet profile needs a thirst block or an explicit exemption. Fluids must give every water block and container a salinity and a foulness, Illness must define what the foul load does, and Village simulation must define what an unloaded village loses to a dry well.

# Plants

The wild plants and trees Feudum adds or changes, and the mechanic that describes each. Some of them also grow in gardens, such as the herbalist's physic garden. Field and garden crops are in the [crops index](../crops/README.md).

## Herbs

| Herb      | Used for                                         | Mechanic                                                                                                    |
|:----------|:-------------------------------------------------|:------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Horehound | Horehound syrup                                  | [Disease](../mechanics/disease.md#medicinal-plants-and-rye)                                                 |
| Mint      | Mint tea                                         | [Disease](../mechanics/disease.md#medicinal-plants-and-rye)                                                 |
| Chamomile | Chamomile tea                                    | [Disease](../mechanics/disease.md#medicinal-plants-and-rye)                                                 |
| Mustard   | Mustard poultice                                 | [Disease](../mechanics/disease.md#medicinal-plants-and-rye)                                                 |
| Wormwood  | Wormwood draught, and herb sachets against moths | [Disease](../mechanics/disease.md#medicinal-plants-and-rye), [Vermin](../mechanics/vermin.md#clothes-moths) |
| Lavender  | Herb sachets against moths                       | [Vermin](../mechanics/vermin.md#clothes-moths)                                                              |
| Rue       | Herb sachets against moths                       | [Vermin](../mechanics/vermin.md#clothes-moths)                                                              |

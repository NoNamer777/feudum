# Mechanics

The game mechanics of Feudum. A mechanic gets its own page in this folder once a grilling session settles its design. The [docs index](../../README.md) lists the other areas, such as world generation and content.

## Designed

- **[Time](time.md)**: the clock, the calendar with its seasons and sub-seasons, climate profiles, and catch-up for unloaded villages.
- **[Villages](villages.md)**: what a village is, its territory and lifecycle, standing, manors, and lordship.
- **[Weather](weather.md)**: fronts that drift across the map, weather values, local temperature, snow and ice, and weather signs.

## Planned

Mechanics that came up during a grilling session but were not its focus. Each waits for a session of its own.

- **Villagers**: the villager model that daily schedules, aging, homes, and village officers run on, including villager lords. Came up in Time and Villages.
- **Village economy**: stock and production, and the harvests, markets, tithes, and taxes on the calendar. Came up in Time.
- **Village simulation**: how a village reaches its decisions, and how it advances as abstract data while its chunks are unloaded. Came up in Time and Villages.
- **Farming**: crops whose growth depends on the season. Came up in Time.
- **Buildings**: how buildings are designed, built, and recognized as part of a village. Came up in Villages.
- **Lordship contests**: how a player challenges a sitting lord, how a contest is won, and what the loser keeps. Came up in Villages.
- **Realms**: fealty between lords, up to a sovereign with no lord above them, which a player can become. Came up in Villages.
- **Body temperature**: what cold, heat, and wetness do to players. Came up in Weather.
- **Skills**: how players and villagers learn and improve skills, such as reading weather signs, through reading (a skill of its own), being taught by villagers, and practice. Came up in Weather.
- **Segmented sleep**: a first and second sleep with a waking hour between, for villager schedules and possibly player sleep. Revisits the sleep speed-up of Time. Came up in Weather.
- **Fire**: how fire catches, spreads, and burns out, and how rain puts it out. Came up in Weather.

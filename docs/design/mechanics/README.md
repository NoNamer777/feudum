# Mechanics

The game mechanics of Feudum. A mechanic gets its own page in this folder once its design is settled. The [design index](../README.md) lists the other areas, such as world generation and content.

## Designed

- **[Animal husbandry](animal-husbandry.md)**: livestock and where it comes from, breeding in its season, pregnancy, birth, nursing, growth, and old age, heritable traits and breeds, fleece and eggs, slaughter and butchering, ownership and strays, herding, draft work, and crowding.
- **[Body temperature](body-temperature.md)**: thermal profiles for every living mob, felt temperature and its inputs, wetness, shelter and room temperature, and the stages of cold and heat.
- **[Brewing](brewing.md)**: malting, ale and beer, cider, perry, wine, and mead, fermentation that makes water safe, distilling, keeping and souring, and drunkenness with its hangover, tolerance, dependence, and withdrawal.
- **[Cooking](cooking.md)**: raw and cooked food, the hearth, heated cauldron, and bake oven, doneness, dish templates that compute their food component from their ingredients, bread and leavening, boiling water, rendering, the warmth and keeping of dishes, and bowls and trenchers.
- **[Fire](fire.md)**: fuel loads and burnt forms, dryness and seasoned firewood, lighting a fire, spread downwind with embers and sparks, putting fire out, fuels and the charcoal clamp, the fireplace, the open fire, and banking, room smoke, and burning bodies and items.
- **[Fluids](fluids.md)**: finite, volumetric water, rivers on a generated drainage network, the water cycle and groundwater, salinity and foulness, and how bodies swim and float.
- **[Food preservation](food-preservation.md)**: keeping times and the stages of spoilage, how temperature, wetness, and containers set the rate, cellars and ice houses, salting, smoking, drying, pickling, potting, and honey preserves, and rotten items.
- **[Health](health.md)**: a pool of health for each body part, where hits land, blood and bleeding, injuries and their treatment, natural healing, the Downed and Unconscious states, and the rule that combines the modifiers of every need.
- **[Hunger](hunger.md)**: diet profiles for every mob that eats, the stomach, energy, and food groups, drain and its stages, deficiencies and cravings, and the feeding goal of animals.
- **[Hygiene](hygiene.md)**: grime, mud, and bloodstain on bodies and garments, smell and scent, washing in basins, tubs, stone baths, and open water, laundry and drying, lye and soap, and dirty animals.
- **[Illness](illness.md)**: named diseases and their course from incubation to immunity, symptoms that act through the needs, care, the foul load and rotting wounds, spread by breath, waste, touch, shared things, and vectors, and the plagues of livestock.
- **[Realms](realms.md)**: fealty between manors, the realm record and its sovereign, tribute, aid, and wish weight up and down the chain, peace in the realm, liege realms, and rank.
- **[Relief](relief.md)**: the bladder and bowels, their stages and accidents, relieving and where waste goes, how waste fouls water and fades, and the dung heap that rots it into manure.
- **[Textiles](textiles.md)**: six fibers from retting and shearing to thread, rope, and cloth, fulling and felt, dyes, overdyeing, and fading, the lant tub, and wall hangings and rugs.
- **[Thirst](thirst.md)**: hydration for every mob that eats, the salt and foul loads, drain and its stages, cues, and the drinking goal of animals.
- **[Time](time.md)**: the clock, the calendar with its seasons and sub-seasons, climate profiles, and catch-up for unloaded villages.
- **[Villages](villages.md)**: what a village is, its territory and lifecycle, standing, manors, and lordship.
- **[Weather](weather.md)**: fronts that drift across the map, weather values, wind, local temperature, snow and ice, and weather signs.

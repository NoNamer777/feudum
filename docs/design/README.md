# Design

The design of Feudum and the decisions behind it, with an index for each area of the game. Each area's index lists its designed topics in alphabetical order, each with a page of its own. The planned topics still waiting for a page are [open design issues](https://github.com/NoNamer777/feudum/issues?q=is%3Aissue%20is%3Aopen%20label%3Adesign).

- **[Crafts](crafts/README.md)**: crafts and tools, and the goods made from raw materials, starting with tools.
- **[Farming](farming/README.md)**: crops, livestock, and working the land, starting with plants.
- **[Needs](needs/README.md)**: what players, villagers, and animals need to stay well, starting with rest.
- **[Setting](setting/README.md)**: the world Feudum is set in, its era and its tone.
- **[World](world/README.md)**: the world Feudum's villages stand in, from its clock and calendar onward.

# Content

The blocks, items, and entities of Feudum, which replace their vanilla counterparts. A piece of content gets its own page in this folder once its design is settled. The [design index](../README.md) lists the other areas.

## Designed

- **[Clothing](clothing.md)**: three layers and a cloak over five regions, garment types and materials, how their stats combine, garment wetness and drying, wear and mending, and soiled garments.

## Planned

Content that came up while designing other topics and wait for a design of their own are [open issues](https://github.com/NoNamer777/feudum/issues?q=is%3Aissue%20is%3Aopen%20label%3Adesign%20label%3Acontent) labeled `design` and `content`.

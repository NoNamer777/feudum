# Content

The blocks, items, and entities of Feudum, which replace their vanilla counterparts. A piece of content gets its own page in this folder once its design is settled. The [design index](../README.md) lists the other areas.

## Designed

None yet.

## Planned

Content that came up while designing other topics and waits for a design of its own.

- **Clothing layers**: whether Feudum adds clothing slots under armor, and the clothing that fills them.
- **Drink containers**: the cup, waterskin, jug, bucket, and barrel that hold drinks. A container keeps the quality of what it holds, and the water in a placed container or unheated storage freezes below 0 °C.
- **Feed trough and hay**: the troughs livestock eat and drink from, and the hay that carries a herd through winter, when snow cover blocks grazing. A water trough fouls over a few days of use, and freezes in winter.
- **Foods**: the food items that replace vanilla's, each with its energy and food groups.
- **Rain barrel**: a barrel that collects clean water from precipitation.
- **Snow and ice**: the Feudum snow layer and ice blocks that weather builds up, drifts, and melts.
- **Timekeeping items**: a Feudum clock and calendar that show the player the time, date, and season.
- **Weather vane**: a vane that shows where the wind blows from. It reads the sheltered wind at its block, so it must sit high and clear to read true.
- **Windmill**: a mill whose milling is powered by the wind speed.

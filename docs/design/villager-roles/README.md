# Villager roles

The roles a villager can hold, such as an office or a title. A role gets its own page in this folder once its design is settled. The planned Villagers mechanic in the [mechanics index](../mechanics/README.md) owns the villager model that roles run on, and the [design index](../README.md) lists the other areas.

## Designed

None yet.

## Planned

Roles that came up while designing other topics and wait for a design of their own.

- **Hayward**: the officer who guards a village's crops and fences.
- **Reeve**: the officer who carries out a village's decisions and runs its daily work.
- **Villager lord**: a villager a manor is sworn to. [Villages](../mechanics/villages.md#lordship) sets its weight and rights; its page will settle who becomes one and what it wishes.
